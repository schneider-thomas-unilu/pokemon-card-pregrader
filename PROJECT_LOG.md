# Digital Transformation: Pokémon Card PreGrader

1) #  Problem Statement:

   Professional trading card resellers must make rapid purchasing decisions, often under pressure and with limited information about a card’s condition. Misjudgments, distractions, or lack of grading expertise can lead to costly mistakes, as submitting a card for professional grading requires both significant financial investment and time. An inaccurate assessment can reduce profit margins or turn a promising purchase into a loss.  
   Our application addresses this challenge by allowing users to scan trading cards and receive a consistent, AI-assisted pre-grading estimate. By suggesting a potential grade and helping assess whether professional grading is financially worthwhile, the application supports faster, more informed purchasing decisions and reduces the risk of costly miscalculations.  
   Furthermore, it lowers the barrier to entry for less experienced resellers, enabling them to evaluate potential purchases more confidently and compete more effectively in the trading card market.

2) # Users and Customers

   The primary users are trading card resellers and professional card dealers who regularly buy ungraded cards, assess their condition, submit promising cards to grading companies such as PSA, TAG, or CGC, and resell them for a profit.  
   The application is especially valuable to:  
* Professional resellers: process many cards and need to make quick, consistent purchasing decisions.  
* Small card-shop owners and independent sellers: need to minimize grading costs and avoid unprofitable purchases.  
* Beginner resellers: lack the experience to accurately estimate a card's potential grade and resale value.  
  The resellers and card businesses themselves would pay for the application, likely through a monthly subscription. They are the direct beneficiaries of fewer costly mistakes, better purchasing decisions, and more efficient card evaluation. Individual collectors could be a secondary customer segment.  
  According to [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/trading-cards-market-report), the global trading cards market was valued at approximately \$30 billion in 2025, with a projected value of \$32.1 billion in 2026\.

4) # Alternatives

The most common alternative is to rely on personal experience and knowledge to assess a card's condition. However, even experienced resellers can overlook minor defects or make mistakes due to distractions and time pressure. Developing reliable grading expertise also takes considerable time, creating a significant barrier to entry for new resellers

Resellers can purchase cards based on their initial impressions and submit them directly for professional grading. Although this approach can be profitable, it exposes buyers to considerable financial risk when the final grade is lower than expected, potentially resulting in lost money and wasted time.

[SnapCard](https://trysnapcard.com/) already offers AI-assisted card pre-grading. However, its iOS-only availability may exclude Android users, representing a potential opportunity for our application. Some user reviews also raise concerns about subscription pricing and grading accuracy. These concerns highlight areas where we could differentiate our product through broader platform support, competitive pricing, and reliable, transparent grade estimates.

5) #  Value proposition

For trading card resellers who need to assess ungraded cards quickly and avoid costly grading mistakes, our application is an AI-powered pre-grading tool that estimates card grades and helps determine whether professional grading is financially worthwhile, unlike manual inspection or existing pre-grading tools that may be inaccessible or insufficiently reliable for their needs. 

6) #  Why AI?

AI enables our application to analyze card images and detect subtle defects, such as scratches, edge wear, corner damage, and centering issues, that may be overlooked during manual inspection. Without AI, evaluating each card would require significant expertise and time, making large-scale, consistent pre-grading difficult and expensive. AI allows us to automate this initial assessment, provide rapid grading estimates, and help resellers make informed purchasing decisions before investing in professional grading. 

7) #  Monetization
3 Tiered Subscription Model:

Tier 1 Independent:
* Target: Casual resellers, small card-shop owners, or beginners scaling up their operations.
* Price: 19 € / month
* Value Justification: If this tier helps a reseller avoid just one unnecessary or low-grade submission every 2 to 3 months, it has already paid for itself.
* Features:
   * Up to 50 AI pre-grades per month
   * Standard condition analysis (surface, corners, edges, centering)
   * Estimated ROI & grading cost-benefit calculator (PSA/CGC fee tables built-in)
Tier 2 Professional:
* Target: Full-time professional resellers processing regular batches of cards weekly.
* Price: 49€ / month
* Value Justification: Exactly matches the cost of one standard PSA Europe grading fee (49.99 €). Avoiding one mistake a month covers the entire monthly subscription
* Features:
   * Up to 250 AI pre-grades per month
   * Advanced high-resolution defect detection (micro-scratches, subtle surface dents)
   * Batch scanning mode (scan multiple cards in succession quickly)
   * Live market pricing integration (estimated raw vs. graded value comparison)
Tier 3 Business Tier:
* Target User: High-volume card shops, team-based operations, or major online sellers.
* Price: 149 € / month (or 1,430 € / year – approx. 119 €/mo billed annually)
* Value Justification: For businesses grading dozens or hundreds of cards a month, preventing even a couple of miscalculations saves hundreds of euros.
* Features:
   * Unlimited (or very high cap, e.g., 1,000+) AI pre-grades per month
   * Multi-user accounts / team access
   * Advanced export options (CSV/Excel inventory tracking for tax and bookkeeping)
   * Priority customer support & feature requests
